Turnkey design-build for villas

Turnkey Villa Projects in Koh Phangan

A turnkey villa project with FORMA covers everything from the first sketch to handover on Koh Phangan: design, permit documentation, construction, interiors and landscape under one contract and one point of responsibility. You decide at the main milestones, and the team manages the work between them.

One brief, one team, one outcome

Coordinating separate architects, interior designers, builders and project managers can work well when every relationship is managed carefully — but it also multiplies the points where miscommunication and cost creep can occur. Our turnkey service removes that overhead for clients who want a single point of accountability: one studio responsible for the design intent, the technical execution and the finished result, from the first concept sketch to the day the keys are handed over.

Scope of work

Integrated design

Architecture, interior and landscape design developed together from the outset, avoiding the misalignment that comes from separate, sequential appointments.

Permits and planning

Approval documentation prepared and coordinated as part of the single project timeline.

Construction delivery

Build executed directly by our construction team against the integrated design documentation.

Project management and handover

Budget, schedule and quality managed end to end, concluding with a structured handover.

One coordinated material and technical strategy

Because architecture, interiors and construction sit within the same team, material decisions are made once and carried through consistently — a stone specified in the architectural drawings is the same stone detailed in the interior joinery and the same stone procured and installed on site, without the translation losses that occur across separate firms.

What a turnkey villa really means

A turnkey villa is a single engagement that runs from the first conversation to a finished home. Design, approvals, construction, interiors and landscape are planned together, under one contract, with one team answerable for the result. You receive a home that is ready to occupy, instead of a collection of separate contracts you have to manage.

Turnkey does not mean you step aside. You approve the brief, the concept, the main material choices and any change that affects cost or schedule. The team handles the coordination between those decisions.

How a turnkey villa project runs, stage by stage

The sequence is deliberate. Early work on the brief, budget and plot sets the limits for everything after it. Design then runs across architecture, interiors and landscape at the same time, so a bathroom layout, a drainage route and a structural column are resolved in one drawing set.

Construction begins once the technical documents and approvals are in place. Fitting out, landscape works and final checks follow, and the project closes with a handover walkthrough.

Who decides what during a turnkey villa project

Clear decision points keep the project from drifting. We present options with their cost and schedule consequences, you choose, and the choice is recorded before the next stage starts. Anything outside the agreed scope is priced and approved before it is built.

This matters most for clients who live abroad. Written reports, photographs and scheduled video calls replace site visits, and a short list of pending decisions tells you what is waiting on you and what it will delay.

Island logistics and climate inside a turnkey scope

On Koh Phangan a turnkey contract absorbs a lot of practical work that clients rarely see. Materials, appliances and furniture cross the water by ferry or barge, and delivery has to match the stage the building has reached. Fittings that arrive too early sit in a humid, dusty site, and fittings that arrive late hold up the crew.

Climate informs the whole package. Heat and humidity affect which timber, metal and fabric choices hold up, salt air limits some fixings, and monsoon rain decides when roofing and exterior finishes can proceed. Because one team owns both the interior and the building, these constraints are handled once instead of being discovered by each trade in turn.

How turnkey connects with FORMA design and build services

A turnkey villa draws on every other service we offer: architecture and villa design at the start, permits and planning in the middle, construction and supervision on site, and project management across the whole timeline. Landscape and interior design are built into the same plan from the beginning.

If you only need part of this, you can still commission design alone or build alone. Turnkey is for clients who want the parts delivered as one continuous project.

What affects turnkey timing and where delays start

We give a schedule after the brief and site survey, not before. The pace depends on plot access, the permit process, structural complexity, imported items and how promptly decisions are made.

Most delays trace back to a few causes: an unclear brief, changes after construction starts, late selection of finishes and approvals that take longer than hoped. Settling finishes and fixtures while the structure is rising is the simplest way to protect the schedule.

Mistakes to avoid when choosing a turnkey villa

Ask what the contract includes and excludes, since turnkey is used loosely and some offers leave out landscape, furniture or approvals. Confirm who is responsible for permit coordination, who pays for variations and how changes are agreed.

Avoid fixing a budget before the site is understood. A steep plot, a long access track or poor ground can alter the plan, so a feasibility review first protects both sides. Also avoid skipping independent oversight if you want a second opinion on quality, which our supervision service can provide.

What affects turnkey project cost

Turnkey budgets reflect the full scope of design and construction together. We build a realistic budget with each client early, rather than presenting a number before the site and brief are properly understood.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is included in a turnkey project?

Architecture, interior design, landscape design, permits coordination, construction and project management, delivered as one continuous engagement through to a completed, furnished-ready home.

How is turnkey different from hiring FORMA for design and construction separately?

The scope can be similar, but turnkey is structured from day one as a single integrated engagement with one budget, one schedule and one point of accountability, rather than sequential separate contracts.

Can we still make design decisions along the way?

Yes — turnkey does not mean handing over blind control. Key decisions at each stage are presented for client approval; the integration is in coordination, not in removing your input.

Is turnkey more expensive than managing separate consultants ourselves?

Not necessarily — coordination overhead, rework from miscommunication and schedule slippage across separate parties often cost more than they appear to save; turnkey pricing reflects the full scope transparently.

Do you handle furniture and move-in styling as part of turnkey?

Yes, furniture specification and final styling are included as part of the interior design scope within a turnkey engagement.

Can turnkey projects include hospitality or multi-villa developments?

Yes, the same integrated approach applies to larger hospitality or multi-unit developments, coordinated closely with our Project Management service for the added complexity.

What is the typical timeline for a turnkey villa?

Timelines depend heavily on scale and specification; we provide a project-specific schedule once the brief and site survey are complete, typically covering design, permits and construction phases sequentially.

How involved do we need to be if we live overseas?

Turnkey is specifically well suited to overseas clients — structured reporting, scheduled video reviews and clearly defined decision points keep the project moving without requiring continuous on-site presence.
