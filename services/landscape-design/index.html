Tropical landscape and outdoor living design

Landscape as an Extension of the House

FORMA designs tropical landscapes on Koh Phangan that integrate pool areas, planting, pathways and outdoor living directly with the architecture, addressing drainage and site slope as design opportunities rather than afterthoughts.

The garden is not what surrounds the house — it completes it

On a tropical island, the boundary between inside and outside is one of the most important design decisions a project makes. A pool positioned without regard to the living room's sightlines, planting that blocks the very breeze the architecture was designed to capture, or drainage left unresolved until construction — these are the failures we design against from the outset, treating landscape as a discipline coordinated with architecture rather than an afterthought applied once the building is finished.

Scope of work

Pool and outdoor living design

Pool positioning, decking and shaded outdoor rooms designed as continuous space with the interior.

Planting strategy

Species selection for climate resilience, privacy, shade and low ongoing maintenance.

Pathways and hardscape

Circulation, steps and retaining details resolved for the site's actual slope and drainage pattern.

Drainage and water management

Surface water strategy that protects the building and prevents erosion on sloped sites during monsoon rainfall.

Planting and materials for tropical gardens

We favour planting palettes that are genuinely resilient to Koh Phangan's conditions — drought-tolerant once established, tolerant of intense sun and heavy seasonal rain, and selected as much for structure and shade as for flowering display. Hardscape materials are chosen for slip resistance around pools and durability against constant UV and moisture exposure.

Process

  1. 01

    Site analysis

    Slope, drainage, existing vegetation and microclimate assessed.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Outdoor living zones and pool positioning developed with the architecture.

  3. 03

    Planting design

    Species palette selected for resilience, privacy and maintenance realism.

  4. 04

    Technical documentation

  5. 05

    Construction coordination

    Landscape works sequenced with building construction where relevant.

  6. 06

    Establishment

    Planting establishment guidance for the first growing seasons.

Deliverables

  • Site and microclimate analysis
  • Landscape concept plan integrated with architecture
  • Planting palette and layout drawings
  • Drainage and hardscape technical detailing
  • Planting establishment and maintenance guidance

What affects landscape design cost

Landscape budgets vary with site scale, slope complexity and the extent of hardscape versus planting in the design.

  • Drainage complexity, particularly on steeper plots
  • Irrigation system requirements for establishment and long-term care

Frequently Asked Questions

Can landscape design be added to an existing villa?

Yes, we regularly design landscape schemes for existing properties, working with the current architecture and site conditions.

How do you handle drainage on steeply sloped sites?

Through a combination of graded surfaces, retaining structures and managed drainage channels designed specifically for the site's contours and monsoon rainfall intensity.

What plants actually thrive on Koh Phangan?

A range of tropical species tolerant of intense sun, heavy seasonal rain and, near the coast, some salt exposure; we select species specific to each site's microclimate rather than a fixed generic list.

Do you design the pool itself or only the surrounding landscape?

Pool positioning and form are typically resolved with the architecture; detailed pool engineering is coordinated with specialist pool contractors alongside our landscape design.

How much maintenance will the garden need?

We design deliberately for realistic maintenance capacity, discussed with the client upfront, favouring resilient planting over high-maintenance formal schemes unless specifically requested.

Can landscape design help with privacy from neighbouring plots?

Yes, strategic planting is one of the most effective and attractive ways to manage privacy without resorting to solid walls that block breeze and views.

Do you handle irrigation system design?

Yes, irrigation is planned as part of the landscape technical documentation, sized appropriately for the planting palette and site conditions.

How long until a new garden looks established?

Tropical planting grows quickly with the right species selection; a garden typically reads as established within one to two growing seasons, depending on the specimen sizes planted initially.
