Independent construction supervision

A Second Set of Eyes on Your Build

FORMA provides independent construction supervision on Koh Phangan for clients building with another contractor, carrying out regular site inspections, quality checks against the design documentation, progress monitoring and structured reporting.

Quality control is not the contractor's job to grade itself

When a client is building with a contractor we did not select and may not be able to visit regularly, an independent set of eyes on-site protects the project from drift — details that quietly diverge from the drawings, workmanship that falls short of specification, or progress that looks fine in photographs but is behind schedule underneath. Our construction supervision service exists specifically for that gap, reporting honestly to the client rather than to the builder.

Scope of work

Site inspections

Scheduled visits at defined construction stages to check work against drawings and specification.

Quality and compliance checks

Verification that materials, workmanship and installed systems match the approved design intent.

Progress monitoring

Independent assessment of actual progress against the contractor's reported schedule.

Snagging and reporting

Defect identification, structured written reports and photographic records for the client's records.

What supervision checks for on site

Supervision inspections focus on the details most likely to cause long-term problems in a tropical climate: waterproofing detailing at roofs, terraces and wet areas, correct concrete cover and curing, structural connection quality, and the accuracy of installed dimensions against drawings — the items a client cannot easily assess from progress photographs alone.

Process

  1. 01

    Onboarding

    Review of contract documents, drawings and current site status.

  2. 02

    Inspection schedule

    Visit frequency agreed around key construction milestones.

  3. 03

    Site visits

    Structured inspections against drawings, specification and workmanship standards.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    Written reports with photographic evidence issued after each visit.

  5. 05

    Issue tracking

    Defects and non-compliance items tracked to resolution.

  6. 06

    Final snagging

    Comprehensive pre-handover inspection and defect list.

Deliverables

  • Inspection schedule aligned to construction milestones
  • Written site reports with photographic evidence
  • Defect and non-compliance tracking log
  • Progress assessment against the contractor's schedule
  • Final snagging report before handover

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supervise a project designed and built entirely by others?

Yes, this is the core use case for independent supervision — protecting a client's interests on a project we did not design or contract to build.

How often do you visit the site during supervision?

Visit frequency is agreed around the project's specific construction milestones, ranging from weekly during critical structural stages to less frequent visits during simpler finishing work.

What happens if you find non-compliant work?

Findings are documented in writing with photographic evidence and communicated to the client directly, along with the recommended remedy to raise with the contractor.

Do you report to the contractor or to the client?

Reporting lines run to the client. Any communication with the contractor about defects happens transparently and is documented, protecting the independence of the supervision role.

Can supervision start partway through an already ongoing build?

Yes, following an initial review of completed work and documentation to establish an accurate baseline before ongoing inspections begin.

Is construction supervision the same as project management?

No — supervision focuses specifically on site quality and design compliance; project management covers the broader coordination of budget, schedule and procurement. See our Project Management service for that scope.

Can supervision reports be shared with our bank or investor?

Yes, structured written reports are commonly shared with lenders or investors requiring independent progress verification.

Do you supervise renovation projects as well as new builds?

Yes, supervision applies equally to renovation works, with particular attention to structural remediation and waterproofing quality.
